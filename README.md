# nhanes-mini-analysis
Self-directed R analysis of NHANES 2017-2018: physical activity and obesity in US adults
In this unweighted analysis of NHANES 2017–2018 adults, those who reported vigorous work activity had higher odds of obesity than those who did not (OR 1.44, 95% CI 1.26–1.65), after adjusting for age and sex. Men had lower odds of obesity than women (OR 0.78, 95% CI 0.70–0.88), and age showed no clear association. This variable measures activity at work and not exercise, so the result probably reflects occupation and related factors such as income and education, which I did not adjust for. 
The design is cross-sectional therefore does not show cause. 
A next step would be to use recreational activity and the survey weights.
